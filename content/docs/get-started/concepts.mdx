Pi Agent Interface has few ideas of its own. This page names them once, so the rest of the docs can use them.

## The agent platform

The agents run on a [Pi Durable platform](https://github.com/ncecere/pi-durable-platform) (pi-durable-platform): it starts each agent's runtime when it is needed, keeps its memory and conversations, and lets an idle agent sleep (hibernate) until the next message. Pi Agent Interface is a front end for it. People sign in to the app, and the app's server reaches the platform for them in one of two ways. In **Connect** mode (the default) each person connects their own platform account once, their agents live in their **personal workspace** on the platform, and the server calls the platform with their own token, so the platform decides what they may use. In **service** mode, for a platform without Connect, one team service account holds everyone's agents and the app checks that each agent is its person's own. See [The agent platform](/docs/operate/platform) and [Connecting your platform account](/docs/use/connect-your-platform-account).

## Agents

An **agent** is a Pi Durable agent on the platform: it has a name, a template it was made from, a model it runs on, its own memory and its own conversations. Every provided agent and every agent you make belongs to exactly one person. Nobody else can open it or its conversations, administrators included. In Connect mode you also see the agents of the teams you belong to on the platform, which are shared with the team.

An agent is **running** or **asleep**. Asleep, it wakes on your next message, which can take a moment ("Waking Assistant…"). An agent can also be **disabled** on the platform by the team's owners; you can't send it messages until it is enabled again.

### Provided agents and your own

| Kind | Who sets it up | What you can do with it |
|---|---|---|
| **Provided** | An administrator, in [Admin → Agents](/docs/administer/agents). Everyone gets their own private copy. The first is the built-in assistant (named **Assistant** unless renamed). | Chat with it. You can't rename, move or delete it. |
| **Yours** | You, in [Settings → My agents](/docs/use/agents), from a template your administrator allows. | Chat with it, rename it, move it to another model (when allowed), and delete it with its conversations. |
| **Team** (Connect mode) | The team's owners, on the platform. | Chat with it. Your conversation with it is private to you. See [Agents](/docs/use/agents#team-agents). |

Your provided agents are made the first time you open the app, and every time the provided list changes, the next time you open it. The agent picker shows them as **Provided** and **Yours**, and then one group for each of your teams.

## Conversations

A **conversation** is a thread with one agent. It stays with the agent it started with, since its memory and tools are that agent's. Its title is the start of your first message until you [rename it](/docs/use/inbox#rename-a-conversation). Conversations are stored on the platform, not in the app's database, and deleting one deletes it from the agent for good.

An agent works on one reply at a time. A message you send while it works waits in a queue and runs after the current reply.

## The inbox

The sidebar is an **inbox** of every conversation with every one of your agents, sorted by what each needs from you:

- **Pinned**, **Working** and **Inbox** are always in view. A conversation whose reply finished or failed since you last looked is marked **Done** or **Failed** and rises to the top.
- **Snoozed**, **Settled** and **Archived** are shelves, closed until you open them.

Whether a conversation is busy, and when its last reply finished or failed, comes from the platform. What you did with it (seen, pinned, snoozed, settled, archived) is yours: it is saved to your account and follows you between devices. See [The inbox](/docs/use/inbox).

## People and roles

Everyone who signs in is a **person** with a role: **Member** or **Administrator**. Administrators have the [admin dashboard](/docs/administer). A person can be **suspended**, which signs them out everywhere and keeps them out until they are restored; their agents and conversations are kept. There is always at least one active administrator.

## Where settings live

| Kind | Where | Who changes it | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Deployment** | Environment variables | Whoever runs the server; a restart applies it | The platform's address and mode, sign-in, `AGENT_MODEL` as a fallback |
| **Instance settings** | The app's database; Admin → Agents, Branding | Administrators, at once, recorded in the audit log | Default model, provided agents, the name and logo |
| **Optional features** | The app's database; Admin → Features | Administrators, at once, recorded in the audit log | Usage (Admin → Usage; service mode only), Personal usage (Settings → Usage) |
| **Personal preferences** | The person's account; Settings | Each person, for themselves, on every device | Theme, accent, avatar style, the inbox |
