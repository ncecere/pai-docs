Two inbox glitches found while capturing the screenshots. [v0.5.0](/docs/releases/v0-5-0) is the latest release.

## Upgrading from 0.3.0

Replace the image and restart. There is no new migration and nothing to change by hand. See [Upgrades and backups](/docs/operate/upgrades-and-backups#from-v030-to-v031). Upgrading from an older version? Read the [v0.3.0](/docs/releases/v0-3-0), [v0.2.1](/docs/releases/v0-2-1) and [v0.2.0](/docs/releases/v0-2-0#upgrading-from-010) notes too.

## What's fixed

- **On a touch screen**, where a row's **More actions** button (…) is always shown, it was drawn over the row's status (**Done**, **Failed**, **Woke**, **Working** with its timer) and its pin. The status now keeps clear of the button.
- **With a row's menu open**, a row's status was drawn under its actions. The status now steps aside while the menu is open or a delete is being confirmed, as it already did on hover and focus.

See [The inbox](/docs/use/inbox#acting-on-a-conversation).
