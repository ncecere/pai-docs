title: Connecting your platform account
description: Coming in Pi Agent Interface v0.2.0 — people connect their own account on the agent platform once, and their agents live in their personal workspace.
---

<Callout type="warn" title="Coming in v0.2.0">
  This is being built for Pi Agent Interface v0.2.0. It isn't in the release these docs describe, and the details below may change before it ships.
</Callout>

Today, Pi Agent Interface reaches everyone's agents with one team service account, and the agents live in that team on the platform (see [The agent platform](/docs/operate/platform)).

In v0.2.0, people will be able to connect their own account on the agent platform instead, through the platform's OAuth Connect:

- **You connect once.**
- **Your agents live in your personal workspace** on the platform.

What else changes, for people and for operators, will be described here when it is decided.
