Every agent you see in Pi Agent Interface is yours alone: nobody else can open it or read its conversations. Each has its own memory and its own conversations, and runs on a model on the agent platform.

## Provided for you

Your administrator decides which agents everyone gets. Each person has their own private copy of each one. The first is the built-in assistant, called **Assistant** unless your administrator renamed it. Provided agents are made for you the first time you open the app, and new ones appear the next time you open it after your administrator adds them.

You can chat with provided agents, but not rename, move or delete them. If your administrator removes one from the list, it disappears from the app.

## My agents

**Settings → My agents** lists **Your agents** (the ones you made) and **Provided for you**. It says how many you have made out of how many you may, for example "2 of 5 made".

### Make an agent

<Steps>
<Step>

Select **New agent**. It is turned off when you have as many as you may ("You have 5, the most allowed. Delete one to make another.") or when no template is allowed.

</Step>
<Step>

Give it a **Name** (up to 60 characters), and choose what to **Start from**: one of the templates your administrator allows, from the agent platform.

</Step>
<Step>

If your administrator lets you choose, pick a **Model**. The default is marked **(default)**. If you can't choose, it runs on the default model.

</Step>
<Step>

Select **Make agent**. "*Research helper* is ready." It appears in the agent picker under **Yours**.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot slot="settings-new-agent" />

If making agents is turned off, the page says "Making agents is turned off for now." and there is no **New agent** button.

### Change or delete one

Each of your agents has three buttons:

- **New chat** starts a conversation with it.
- **Edit** renames it, or moves it to another model when you may choose. A new model applies from the agent's next start; a reply in progress isn't interrupted.
- **Delete** asks first: "The agent, its memory and all its conversations are deleted for good. This can't be undone."

## Avatars

Every agent has an animated avatar in its own colour that shows what it is doing: idle, thinking, working (a tool is running), waiting (reconnecting), needs you (a reply you haven't seen), done, error, and asleep. A disabled agent's avatar is greyed out. The avatar is decorative: its name and status are always written beside it.

There are three styles, **Face**, **Signal** and **Pixel**. Choose yours under **Avatar** in **Appearance settings** or in [Settings → Customization](/docs/use/settings#customization).

## When an agent isn't available

- **Asleep**: the agent's runtime is hibernating to save resources. It wakes on your next message ("Waking *Assistant*…").
- **Disabled**: the team's owners turned it off on the platform. Its message box says "*Assistant* is disabled." and it is left out of the agent picker. If all your agents are disabled, the start page says "Your agents are all disabled. Ask a team owner to enable one."
- **Deleted on the platform** by the team's owners: one of your own agents disappears from My agents.
