## Finding your way

<Screenshot slot="inbox-sidebar" />

- **The sidebar**, on the left: **New Chat**, **Search conversations...**, then your conversations, and your name at the bottom. Your name opens a menu with **Settings**, **Sign out** and, for administrators, **Admin dashboard**. The sidebar's edge can be dragged to make it wider or narrower (double-click it to reset); **Close sidebar** hides it.
- **The conversation**, in the middle, with the message box at the bottom.
- **Appearance settings**, the button at the top right: **Theme** (Light, Dark, System), **Avatar** (Face, Signal, Pixel), **Wrap long code lines**, **Enter adds a new line**, and **All settings**.

With the sidebar closed, three buttons at the top left take its place: **Open sidebar**, **Search commands and agents**, and **New chat** (in a conversation, a new chat with the same agent).

## What your administrator decides

Some things you might expect to set yourself are decided for everyone:

- **Which provided agents you have**, what they are for and which model they run on.
- **How many agents you may make yourself**, from **which templates**, and **whether you may choose their model**.
- **Which optional features are on**, such as [your own usage](/docs/use/settings#usage).
- **The app's name, logo and default look**. Your own theme and accent in Settings win over the default.

If something described here is missing for you, it has been turned off, not broken. Settings ends with the reminder: your administrator can change your role, restore a suspended account, and choose which agents and models are available.

## On a phone

Pi Agent Interface works on a phone. On screens narrower than 1024 pixels the sidebar becomes a panel opened with **Open sidebar** at the top left, with **New Chat** and your menu at its foot; choosing a conversation closes it, and so does Escape. On a touch screen each conversation's **More actions** button (…) is always shown, since there is no pointer to hover with, and its menu has everything. The row's status and pin keep clear of the button.
