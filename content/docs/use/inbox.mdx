title: The inbox
description: How Pi Agent Interface's sidebar sorts every conversation by what it needs from you, how long lists are drawn a page at a time, and how to pin, snooze, settle, archive, rename, search (titles, and messages when your administrator allows) and delete conversations.
---

The sidebar is one list of every conversation with every one of your agents, sorted by what each needs from you. Agents keep working while you are elsewhere, so the inbox is how you notice a reply that finished, a reply that failed, or a snooze that ran out.

## Sections

| Section | What is in it |
|---|---|
| **Pinned** | Conversations you pinned, in the order you pinned them. |
| **Working** | Conversations whose agent is writing a reply now, with a timer. |
| **Inbox** | Everything else. Conversations that need you come first, then the most recent activity. Empty, it says **All caught up.** |
| **Snoozed** | A shelf: conversations hidden until a time you chose, soonest first. |
| **Settled** | A shelf: conversations you are finished with, most recently settled first. |
| **Archived** | A shelf: conversations put away until you restore them. |

The shelves start closed, with a count, for example **Snoozed (2)**; select one to open it. This browser remembers which are open. A closed shelf still shows the conversation you have open, so it never vanishes from under you.

When you have more than one agent, buttons above the list (**All**, then each agent) show only one agent's conversations.

## Long lists

A long list is drawn a page at a time. Each of **Pinned**, **Working**, **Inbox**, **Snoozed**, **Settled** and **Archived** shows its first **40** conversations. When there are more, the list ends with a button such as **Show 40 more · 125 not shown**; select it to add 40 more (the last time, the number that is left). The conversation you have open always stays in its list, wherever it is in the order, so it never disappears because it is further down.

What is not drawn is still there, and a search still looks through **all** of your conversations, not just the ones drawn. Starting a search, or choosing another agent above the list, starts again from the first page.

## A row

Each row in Pinned, Working and Inbox has three lines: the agent's avatar and name, the conversation's title (brighter when there is something new in it), and how long ago something happened (**now**, **5m**, **3h**, **2d**). A pin marks pinned rows. Rows on the shelves are one line: the title, and the time left on a snooze or how long ago it was settled.

The corner of a row says what the conversation needs:

| Status | Means |
|---|---|
| **Working**, with a timer | The agent is writing a reply. The row is dimmed until you look at it. |
| **Done** | A reply finished since you last looked. |
| **Failed** | A reply failed since you last looked (the platform's reason is shown in the conversation), or the agent dropped your message without answering: it is idle, your message is the last in the conversation, and more than 90 seconds have passed since it started working. |
| **Woke** | A snooze ended and you haven't looked yet. |

Done and Failed are the conversations that need you. The browser tab's title counts them, for example **(2) Pi Agent Interface**. Opening the conversation clears its status.

Whether an agent is working, and when its last reply finished or failed, comes from the platform as it happens, whichever device or tab started the work: the app follows the platform's event stream, and lists change in place without reloading. A reply that finished while the app was closed shows as **Done** when you come back. Only while that stream is down does the app ask the platform now and then instead.

## Acting on a conversation

Point at a row to see **Pin**, **Snooze**, **Settle** and **More actions** (…). They appear in place of the row's status and pin. Right-clicking a row opens the same menu as **More actions**. On a touch screen, **More actions** is always shown, with room kept for it so it doesn't cover the row's status or pin, and its menu has everything. While a row's menu is open, or a delete is being confirmed, the row's status and pin step aside.

<Screenshot slot="inbox-row-menu" />

| Action | Does |
|---|---|
| **Pin** / **Unpin** | Keeps it at the top. Pinning also brings it back from Snoozed, Settled or Archived. |
| **Snooze** | Hides it until a time: **In 1 hour**, **In 3 hours**, **This evening** (6 pm, while that is more than an hour away), **Tomorrow morning** (9 am), **Next week** (Monday, 9 am), or **Custom…**. Each shows the time it wakes. |
| **Settle** | Puts it on the Settled shelf: you are finished with it. Settling lets go of a pin and a snooze. |
| **Mark unread** | Marks it **Done** again, as if you hadn't seen the last reply. |
| **Archive** | Puts it on the Archived shelf. |
| **Rename…** | Gives it a name of your own. See below. |
| **Delete…** | Deletes it from the agent for good. A red **Delete** appears in the row; select it within a few seconds to confirm. |

**Settle** and **Archive** are turned off while the agent is working: stop it first. Settling, snoozing or archiving the conversation you have open moves you on to the next one in the list, or to a new chat.

**Pin**, **Unpin**, **Snooze**, **Settle** and **Archive** say what they did, with **Undo** for a few seconds. **Delete** can't be undone.

### How conversations come back

- **Snoozed** ones come back at their time, or **sooner if the agent finishes or fails** in the meantime: a snooze hides a conversation, not what it has to say. **Wake now** brings one back early.
- **Settled** ones come back to the Inbox when anything new happens in them: a message, a finished reply. **Un-settle** brings one back by hand.
- **Archived** ones stay put whatever happens, until you **Restore** them.

To restore or delete many settled or archived conversations at once, use [Settings → Conversations](/docs/use/settings#conversations), which pages its lists the same way.

## Rename a conversation

A conversation's title starts as the beginning of your first message. **Rename…** opens **Rename conversation**: type a name of up to 200 characters and select **Rename**. The name shows in your sidebar and search at once, on every device.

## Search

**Search conversations...** at the top of the sidebar filters your conversations by title as you type, across every section and shelf, including all the conversations that aren't drawn yet. Escape clears it. When nothing matches, the Inbox says **No conversations match.**

While you search, the **Settled** and **Archived** shelves, and **Snoozed**, open by themselves if they hold a match, so a match isn't hidden behind a closed shelf. A shelf with no match isn't shown. If a list has more than 40 matches, the rest are behind **Show more**.

Search looks at titles, and, when your administrator has turned on [message search](#search-inside-messages), at what was said too.

### Search inside messages

When your administrator has turned on **Search message text** (see [Features](/docs/administer/features#search-message-text)), a search of two or more letters or digits also looks inside messages. Results appear in a section **In messages**, above **Pinned**, once you stop typing for a moment. Each is a conversation with its title and a line of the message where the words were found, with the words that matched marked. Select one to open it.

- **It matches the start of words.** Each word you type is matched as the beginning of a word: "expen" finds "expense". Every word you type has to be in the conversation, but not next to each other. Case doesn't matter. Only letters and digits count, and the first eight words are used.
- **A conversation found by its title isn't repeated** in **In messages**; it shows in its own place in the list. If you filter by one agent above the list, only that agent's conversations show.
- **Only conversations you have opened in this app.** The app keeps its own searchable copy of a conversation's text as you read it here. A conversation you have never opened in this app isn't found by its messages (its title still is). The sidebar says so: "Only conversations opened in this app are searched."
- **Your own only.** You search your conversations, nobody else's, and nobody else can search yours. What is searched is what you and the agent said, not the agent's thinking or tool output.
- **Deleting** a conversation here deletes its searchable copy too.
- While it searches, the section says **Searching…**; when nothing is found it says **No messages match.**

If **In messages** never appears, message search is off for your instance.

To start a chat or run a command from the keyboard, use the [command palette](/docs/use/keyboard-shortcuts#the-command-palette) (⌘ K or Ctrl K).

## On every device

What you do in the inbox (pinned, snoozed, settled, archived, read) is saved to your account, so it follows you to every browser and device you sign in on. If you change the same conversation on two devices, the later change wins, removals (an unpin, say) included. The shelves you have open, and the width of the sidebar, are remembered per browser.
