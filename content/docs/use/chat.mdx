## Starting a conversation

**New Chat** opens the start page: "What should *Assistant* work on, *Priya*?", with the agent's avatar, what it is for, and whether it is **Running** or **Asleep: wakes on your next message**.

- On a wide screen, a row of buttons below the question switches between your agents (up to eight), and **Recent with *agent*** lists your five latest conversations with it.
- **Make your own agent** goes to [Settings → My agents](/docs/use/agents).
- The agent's name at the bottom left of the message box opens the **agent picker**: search your agents (by name, description, team or template). They are grouped, **Provided** first, then **Yours**, then one group for each of your teams (in Connect mode; see [Agents](/docs/use/agents#team-agents)). The group headings show when there is more than one group. **Make or manage your agents** at its foot goes to My agents.

<Screenshot slot="chat-agent-picker" />

Type in the message box and press Enter (Shift+Enter starts a new line; you can [swap these](/docs/use/settings#customization)). The conversation opens at once and your message is sent from there, so an agent that has to wake up shows "Waking *Assistant*…" rather than a frozen page. A new chat starts with the agent you asked for, else the one you used last, else your first.

A conversation stays with the agent it started with; in a conversation, the agent's name in the message box can't be changed. To ask another agent, start a new chat. A conversation you start with a team's agent is private to you (see [Agents](/docs/use/agents#team-agents)).

## While the agent works

The reply streams in as it is written: Markdown with headings, lists, tables, syntax-highlighted code, maths and diagrams. Above the answer, a block shows the agent's reasoning and tool steps; see [Tool steps and reasoning](/docs/use/tool-steps-and-reasoning).

- **Queue a message.** You can keep typing while the agent works. The message box says "The agent is working. Your message will run after the current reply.", the send button becomes **Queue message**, and your message shows **Queued: the agent takes it next.** until it does.
- **Stop.** The square button beside the message box, **Stop the agent**, ends the reply. What was written so far is kept.
- **Leave.** You can open another conversation or close the tab; the agent keeps working on the platform. The conversation shows as **Working** in the [inbox](/docs/use/inbox) and as **Done** when its reply finishes.
- **Jump to latest.** If you scroll up while a reply is written, the view stays where you are and a **Jump to latest** button appears.

A message can be up to 32,000 characters; a longer one is refused with its length. Long messages you sent (more than 12 lines, or 1,200 characters) fold, with **Show more**. Sending is safe to retry: a message that reached the platform once is never sent twice.

## The connection

A conversation follows the agent live. If the connection drops, it says **Reconnecting to the agent…** and reconnects on its own. When a tab has been in the background for a minute and the agent isn't working, the conversation lets go of the agent so it can sleep, and says **Paused while this tab was in the background.** with **Resume**; it picks up again when you come back to the tab.

If the platform connection has ended (Connect mode), the app shows **Connect your agents** instead: see [Connecting your platform account](/docs/use/connect-your-platform-account#when-the-connection-ends).

If the conversation can't be opened, the page says **Conversation unavailable** (deleted, or not one of your agents) or **Could not open the conversation**, with **Retry** and **New chat**.

## When a reply fails

A failed reply says so in its place, in plain words, with **Try again**:

> **This reply failed.** The model is busy or over its limit right now. Try again in a minute.

**Details** shows the model provider's error as it came, for whoever has to look into it. The sentences cover a conversation too long for the model, a model that is busy or over its limit, a model that took too long, a model the platform was refused access to, and a request the model's provider rejected.

If the agent stops without answering at all, a note under your message says **No reply.** *Assistant* stopped without answering your last message, with **Try again**. In the inbox, a turn the agent dropped shows **Failed** rather than Working.

**Try again** and **Send again** (under a reply) put your last question back in the message box, so you can send it as it is or change it first.

## What you can do with a message

Point at a message to see its buttons (on a phone they are always shown):

| Button | On | Does |
|---|---|---|
| **Copy message** | Any message | Copies its text. |
| **Send again** | The latest reply | Puts the question it answered back in the message box. |

Links in a reply that lead to another site open through **Open external link**, which names the site and asks you to **Continue**. **Don't show this warning again** turns it off in this browser.
