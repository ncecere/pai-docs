**Admin → Agents** decides which agents people have and what they run on. The agents live on the agent platform. The models and templates offered here are what the platform offers the app:

- **In service mode**, the models published to the app's team, and the platform's templates and the team's own.
- **In Connect mode**, what a **personal workspace** may use, as the platform shows it to you: the models and templates listed are the ones **your own workspace** offers, as a guide. Each person gets what theirs offers, and an agent that can't be made in someone's workspace is skipped for them. The page says so: "The agents live on the agent platform, in each person’s own workspace. The models and templates below are the ones your own workspace offers, as a guide: each person gets what theirs offers, and an agent that cannot be made in someone’s workspace is skipped for them." The page reads it with your own connection, so you connect your platform account like everyone else, and the templates are the platform's catalog (personal workspaces can deploy catalog templates only). See [Connecting your platform account](/docs/use/connect-your-platform-account).

If the platform can't be reached, the page says so instead. The settings below are the same in both modes.

## Everyone's agents

| Setting | Default | Does |
|---|---|---|
| **Default model** | `AGENT_MODEL`, or the platform's default | What agents run on unless chosen otherwise. The list holds the models published to the team and enabled. Unset, it falls back to `AGENT_MODEL` (shown as **AGENT_MODEL (*name*)**) and then to **Platform's default**. |
| **Agents each person may make** | 5 | Their own agents, besides the provided ones: 0 to 50. **0** turns making agents off. In Connect mode the platform has its own limit on a personal workspace (its `maxAgents`), and **the stricter of the two wins**: someone under your limit can still be refused by the platform's. |
| **People choose the model** | On | For agents they make. Off: those run on the default model, and people can't move them to another. |
| **Templates people may make agents from** | **Every template, including ones added later** | Templates come from the agent platform: its catalog, and in service mode your team's own (marked **· your team**). Untick **Every template…** to choose some. |

**Save** applies the changes; **Discard** drops them.

### How the default model is applied

- New agents are made on the default model, unless a provided agent or the person chose another.
- Each person's copies of the provided agents are moved to the model they should be on (the provided agent's own, else the default) the next time that person opens the app. The change takes effect from the agent's next start; an agent at work isn't interrupted. Agents people made themselves keep the model they were made on until their person changes it.
- Only a model that is published to the team and enabled is ever set (in Connect mode, one that the person's own personal workspace may use): an agent on an unpublished model wouldn't start. If the model can't be found, agents are left as they are, and the server's log says so once. If a move fails, it is logged and tried again five minutes later; nobody is kept from their agent.

The models and templates are read from the platform at most every five minutes, so a model just published may take that long to appear.

## Provided agents

Agents everyone gets. Each person has their own copy, with private conversations. In Connect mode a copy is made **once per person, in their personal workspace**, the first time they open the app after it is added; if the workspace already has an agent under that name, for example from an earlier attempt, it is adopted rather than made again. The first is the built-in assistant (**· built in**), whose name comes from `AGENT_NAME` (default **Assistant**) until you change it here. It can be renamed and changed but not removed.

**Add a provided agent** (up to 20 in all) or the pencil on a row opens the editor:

| Field | Does |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Up to 60 characters, for example "HR Helper". |
| **What it's for** | Up to 500 characters, shown under its name, for example "Questions about benefits and leave". |
| **Template** | A template from the platform, or **The default template** (the template named by `AGENT_TEMPLATE`, else the platform's default). In Connect mode the template must be in the platform's catalog (see below). |
| **Model** | A published model, or **The default model**. |

<Screenshot slot="admin-provided-agent" />

Changes reach each person's copy the next time they open the app; a new model applies from the agent's next start. The name and description people see change with it. A copy is made from its template once, when it is first made (the platform keeps an existing agent as it is), so a change of template applies only to copies made after it.

Removing a provided agent (the bin on its row) takes it out of the app for everyone: "*HR Helper* removed. People's existing copies stay on the platform until deleted there." In service mode the team's owners can delete them in the platform's console. In Connect mode the copies are in people's own workspaces, and a copy the app no longer knows as provided shows up under **Yours** in its person's agent picker, and counts toward their limit, until they delete it.

### Provided agents need catalog templates in Connect mode

A personal workspace can only deploy templates from the platform's **catalog**. If a provided agent names a template that isn't there, the app can't make its copy: it skips that agent, and the server's log says `provided agent "HR Helper" uses a template that isn't in the platform's catalog; personal workspaces can't deploy it`. Publish the template to the catalog on the platform, or choose another. Provided agents left on **The default template** use `AGENT_TEMPLATE` if it is a slug in the catalog, and otherwise the platform's default.

## On the platform

**In service mode**, each agent the app makes is named for the team's owners, who see every person's agent in the platform's console: "*Assistant* · *Priya Shah*", with the person's email in its description. The team's owners can disable or delete agents there. A person's own agent deleted on the platform disappears from their My agents.

**In Connect mode**, agents are in each person's personal workspace, named as the app names them ("*Assistant*"), and are not in a team that you or the platform's team owners see. Agents the person made in the platform's own console in their workspace appear in their My agents too.
