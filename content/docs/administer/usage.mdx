**Admin → Usage** shows how much each person's agents use their models over the last **7 days**, **30 days** or **90 days**. In service mode it is an [optional feature](/docs/administer/features), **Usage**, on by default; while it is off, the page isn't in the admin page list.

<Callout title="Two variants">
  In service mode this page reads the team's usage report, and is the **Usage** feature (on by default). In Connect mode **Usage** is locked off, since people's agents are in their own personal workspaces, and the page is instead the **Usage of connected people** feature (off by default): [Features](/docs/administer/features#usage-of-connected-people) says what it reads. The sections below are about service mode; [In Connect mode](#in-connect-mode) says what is different.
</Callout>

<Screenshot slot="admin-usage" />

The page opens with **Requests**, **Tokens** (in and out), **Estimated cost** and **People** whose agents were used, then **Tokens per day** as bars, then a table **By person**: **Agents** used, **Requests**, **Tokens in**, **Tokens out** and **Est. cost**, the heaviest users first.

## Where the numbers come from

The figures come from the agent platform's usage report for the app's team (`GET /v1/teams/{team}/usage`), read each time the page opens. Every agent the app makes carries a key that names its person, so the platform's rows are summed per person. Rows that name nobody here (agents made in the team by hand, deleted agents, or a person no longer here) are summed as **Other agents**.

- **Days are in UTC**, ending today.
- **The cost is an estimate** from the prices the platform has for each model. A dash means none of the requests were to priced models; a **+** after a cost means some requests weren't priced and aren't in it. Cost appears once the platform has prices for the models.

If the platform's report can't be read, the page says "The usage report could not be read" and why. A platform without the usage report is a reason to lock the feature off with `DISABLED_FEATURES=usage`.

## In Connect mode

With [Connect](/docs/operate/platform) the page is the **Usage of connected people** feature, which an administrator turns on in [Admin → Features](/docs/administer/features#usage-of-connected-people); until then the page isn't in the admin page list. It looks the same, with these differences:

- **Where the numbers come from.** There is no team report to read, so the app reads each connected person's own report from the platform, by agent and by day, **with that person's own connection**, when you open the page, a few people at a time. Each person's report is kept for a minute, so reopening the page is quick, and a change in the platform's figures can take up to a minute to show.
- **Only agents in people's personal workspaces.** What people run in their teams isn't counted. The page's description says so: "Agents of their teams are not counted."
- **Who isn't covered is listed apart.** A fifth figure, **Not covered** ("not connected, or not reachable"), counts the people who aren't in the figures, and the list **Not in these figures** names them, with "Their use is unknown here, which is not the same as none." Each shows either **Has not connected**, or **Could not be read** with the reason (for example "The platform answered 503." or "The platform could not be reached."), for a person whose connection ended just now or whose report the platform wouldn't give. They are never counted as zero, and one person's failure doesn't lose the others.
- **Someone connected who used nothing** has no row in **By person**. There is no **Other agents** row.
- Days are in UTC and the estimated cost works as above.

## People's own usage

With **Personal usage** on, everyone sees their own figures in [Settings → Usage](/docs/use/settings#usage), per agent and per day, named as they know their agents. Nobody sees anyone else's.
